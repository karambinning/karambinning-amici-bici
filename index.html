№ 01 · About

Friends. Bikes. Pasta.

Heeley locals, road-bike pilgrims, Sunday-walkers and people who've cycled up Abbeydale Road specifically for the espresso — they all end up at the same tables.

Amici & Bici opened its doors on Abbeydale Road in 2013 with a simple idea: an honest Italian kitchen, a great cup of coffee, and walls full of the things we love — cycling jerseys cut into bunting, paintings of climbs and breakaways, beautifully restored bikes hung where the artwork would usually go. The name means friends and bikes, and it pretty much covers what happens here.

By day we're a cafe. Coffee is locally roasted by Grumpy Mule and pulled properly. Breakfast runs all week and the full English is, by polite local consensus, not optional. Lunch leans on fresh pasta, panini cut from a real loaf, soups that change with the week, and cake from the counter.

Thursday to Saturday the kitchen stays open into the evening as a proper restaurant: wood-fired stone-baked pizza, plates of pasta and risotto, a short list of desserts that always includes the strawberry tiramisù we'd riot if we removed. There's a side garden facing Chippinghouse Road with covered seating and a generous run of bedding plants, which has saved many a Sheffield summer evening.

We're independent, we live in the neighbourhood, and we'd rather you booked a table than waited in the rain. Call us — that's what the phone is for.

№ 02 · The Menu

What's on, most days.

A working sketch of what we serve. The kitchen tweaks specials weekly and the pizza board changes when the seasons turn. Ring through for the latest if you're chasing something specific.

Breakfast all week · from 8am

  • Full English
    The classic — bacon, sausage, eggs, beans, mushrooms, tomato, toast. Polite local consensus: not optional.
  • Italian breakfast
    Cured meats, eggs your way, ciabatta, plum tomatoes, espresso. Buongiorno.
  • Eggs & toast
    Done properly. Real loaf, runny yolk, salted butter.

From the bar grumpy mule beans

  • Espresso · Cappuccino · Latte
    Pulled properly. Locally roasted. The reason a lot of you keep coming back.
  • Macchiato · Americano · Flat white
    However you take it.
  • Italian sodas & soft drinks
    Chinotto, aranciata, San Pellegrino, the usual lemonades.

Lunch & pasta noon – late afternoon

  • Fresh pasta of the week
    A rotating ragù, a vegetarian option, sometimes a seafood plate. Ask the counter.
  • Panini & ciabatta
    Properly filled. Cured meats, mozzarella, roasted veg, the good stuff.
  • Soup of the day
    Made on the morning. Changes with the week and the weather.

Evening · pizza & plates thu · fri · sat

  • Wood-fired stone-baked pizza
    A short, changing board. Hand-stretched bases. Eat in or take out.
  • Pasta & risotto
    Plated up properly. A vegetarian, a meat, a seafood — most weeks.
  • Strawberry tiramisù
    The one we'd lose customers over if we ever took it off. We won't.
  • Chocolate mousse
    Dense, dark, served cold. A staple.
